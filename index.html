About the Initiative

Our Work for Street Animals

Compassion works best when it's practical. Our animal welfare initiative focuses on the everyday, tangible support street animals need — food in their bellies, shelter from the weather, medical care when they're hurt, and families willing to bring them home.

🍲

Feeding

Regularly providing food and water to stray dogs across our feeding routes.

🏠

Shelter

Supporting and creating safe spaces and dog houses for animals to rest and stay dry.

🏥

Rescue & Medical Care

Responding to animals that are injured, sick, or in urgent need of attention.

🏡

Adoption

Connecting rescued animals with responsible, loving adopters for the long term.
